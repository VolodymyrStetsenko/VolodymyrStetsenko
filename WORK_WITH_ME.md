# Working With Me

I run a founder-led security research and engineering practice for teams building systems where failures can cross code, permissions, external tools, proof boundaries, and on-chain execution.

## Who I Work With

- protocol founders and engineering leads preparing a defined release;
- security teams reviewing smart-contract, verifier, or cross-system trust boundaries;
- teams deploying AI agents with tools, sensitive data, code execution, or transaction authority;
- teams reconstructing an on-chain incident from incomplete or conflicting evidence.

## Engagements

### Protocol Security Review

For smart contracts, protocol components, and proof-verifier integrations with a fixed revision and explicit scope.

Typical work includes:

- architecture and trust-boundary mapping;
- authorization, accounting, state-transition, callback, upgrade, and integration analysis;
- invariant definition and adversarial test design;
- proof-to-application statement binding and replay analysis where applicable;
- reproducible findings with affected paths, impact, evidence, and remediation guidance;
- remediation verification against the reviewed revision.

### Agentic Systems Security Review

For AI systems that use tools, retrieve untrusted content, execute code, access sensitive data, or propose actions with real-world consequences.

Typical work includes:

- agent, tool, identity, memory, and authority mapping;
- indirect prompt-injection and untrusted-context testing;
- tool-abuse, data-exposure, goal-hijacking, and authority-escalation analysis;
- policy, approval, transaction, and human-oversight boundary review;
- evidence capture and reproducible adversarial cases;
- prioritized hardening and regression-test recommendations.

This work is scoped to observable system behavior and explicit permissions. It is not a certification that a model or agent is universally safe.

### Incident Reconstruction

For confirmed on-chain events that require a bounded, evidence-led technical record.

Typical work includes:

- transaction chronology and execution-path reconstruction;
- committed and attempted asset-movement analysis;
- contract, proxy, implementation, and state context;
- root-cause hypotheses separated from observed evidence;
- provenance, coverage limits, contradictory evidence, and unresolved gaps;
- a portable technical report and supporting artifacts.

Public-ledger proximity does not prove identity, intent, ownership, or common control. Attribution is excluded unless supported by independent evidence.

## Engagement Models

| Model | Appropriate when |
| --- | --- |
| Fixed-scope review | The system, revision, exclusions, and delivery window can be defined before work begins |
| Research retainer | A team needs recurring adversarial analysis, design review, or security research across releases |
| Incident engagement | A confirmed event requires time-bounded reconstruction and an explicit evidence record |

## Delivery Standard

Every engagement begins with a written scope and ends with explicit limitations. Depending on the work, deliverables may include:

- scope, revision, assumptions, and exclusions;
- threat model or invariant map;
- review ledger and prioritized findings;
- reproducible tests, traces, or evidence references;
- technical report and executive summary;
- remediation review.

No review guarantees the absence of vulnerabilities or future incidents.
